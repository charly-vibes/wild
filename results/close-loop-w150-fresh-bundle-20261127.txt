## 3. Wild-150 ground truth vs fresh-bundle wild verdicts

The compile oracle (experiments/compile_oracle_results.txt, rustc 1.98.1) established cargo
ground truth for the 17 disputed genesis transitions: 14 cargo-incompatible-but-accretion cells
all compile (probe PASS), i.e. cargo's caret rule over-declares breaks; 3 transitions have no
consumer demand. The fresh-bundle wild emits no verdicts on any of these pairs: with
inventory incomplete, v1 refuses rather than invents (never-invent-complete-contract). So the
loop currently gates UPSTREAM of the cargo-agreement question: cargo says "compatible", wild
says "cannot complete the contract inventory".

## 4. Where the unlock lives (unchanged, now measured on more evidence)

1. wild-8j4: method-call demand on non-bounded receivers — visible verbatim in wild's own
   fresh bundle (`self.added.is_empty()` in src/bump.rs).
2. wild-90d: std/external-crate awareness for demand resolution — the unsupported-construct
   share of the 150-slot gap.
3. Both shipped as design proposals today (openspec/changes/widen-method-receiver-demands,
   add-external-crate-awareness); implementation would re-run this exact audit (`--crate` x3)
   as the acceptance measurement.
